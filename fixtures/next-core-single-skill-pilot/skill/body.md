# Evidence Credit

## why: purpose

Route one work or verify intent without treating remembered or self-reported success as current proof. The generated entry maps the token to shared canon and one stage reference; a verify Decision owns only runtime evidence behavior.

## guard: work-prose-isolation

Keep the work intent inside its prose lane. Explain that verifier acquisition, dispatch, and result recording are outside the selected lane.

## stage: work

Judgment: Open only `references/work.md` for the selected prose lane and apply its cited canon anchors to the current task.

Why: Work guidance remains useful even when verifier facts are absent or inaccessible.

## stage: channel

Judgment: Open only `references/verify.md`; preserve uncertainty and let the Decision describe any available effect plan.

Why: Channel availability and result presence are observed facts, not proof of a verifier verdict.

## stage: acquire

Judgment: Open only `references/verify.md`; keep domain execution outside the runtime and preserve the exact result data returned by the verifier.

Why: A projected plan is not evidence that its effects occurred or that a result was recorded.

## stage: evidence

Judgment: Open only references/verify.md; credit a pass only under its declared-column rule, and treat reentry: rejudge as agent reentry that freshly collects facts before rejudging.

Why: The task, snapshot, exact selection locator and selected-byte hash, continuation, and currentness must correlate before a pass is creditable; a finding remains non-pass.

## role: verifier

Return the exact task, snapshot, selection locator, selected-byte SHA-256, continuation, verdict, and recorded currentness you observed. Inspect only the supplied inputs, include concrete evidence, and do not write project state.
